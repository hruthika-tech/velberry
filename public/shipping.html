Shipping & Returns

When will my order ship?

Every charm is finished by hand, so orders are usually packed within 2–4 working days. You'll get an email the moment your parcel is handed to the courier, with a tracking number you can follow on our tracking page.

Delivery charges

Delivery charges are shown in your bag and confirmed at checkout for your PIN code before you pay. Orders above the free-delivery amount shown in your bag ship free anywhere we deliver in India.

Damaged or wrong item?

Clay is delicate! If anything arrives broken or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we'll send a replacement or a full refund.

Cancellations & refunds

You can cancel any order that hasn't been shipped yet — just contact us with your order number. Refunds go back to your original payment method (UPI, card or netbanking), usually within 5–7 working days.

Undelivered parcels

If the courier can't reach you after several attempts, the parcel returns to our studio and we'll contact you to arrange re-delivery or a refund.
