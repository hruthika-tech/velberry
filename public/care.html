Caring for your charms

Your charm is baked polymer clay sealed under a glossy glaze — sturdy enough for everyday adventures, but it still loves a little gentleness.

Everyday tips

  • Keep it away from sharp keys rubbing against the glaze — a separate ring for heavy keys helps.
  • Avoid leaving it in hot cars or long, direct sunlight; heat can soften the glaze.
  • Wipe with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid perfume, sanitiser and nail-polish remover.
  • Take it off before swimming or bathing.

The metal bits

Our rings and chains are gold-tone alloy. Keep them dry to keep the shine; if a jump ring opens, gently twist (don't pull) it closed with flat pliers.
