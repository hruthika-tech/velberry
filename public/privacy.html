Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what personal information , trading as CLAYSLAY (www.clayslay.in) collects when you shop with us, why, and who it is shared with. We only collect what we need to make, ship and support your order.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, any gift note, and what you bought.
  • Account details (only if you create an account): your name, email, phone, saved addresses and a securely hashed password — we never store your password itself.
  • Payment status: whether your payment succeeded, the amount and the payment reference. We never receive or store your card details, UPI PIN or bank login — payments are handled entirely by Razorpay.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in and remembers your bag, and basic server logs (such as IP address and time) used for security and to prevent abuse.

Why we use it

  • To take your payment, make and pack your order, and deliver it.
  • To email you about your order — confirmation, shipping updates and refunds. We don't send marketing emails.
  • To answer your questions, handle returns and refunds, and keep the records the law requires.
  • To keep the shop secure (for example, limiting repeated login attempts).

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the shop, and only what each one needs:

  • Razorpay — processes your payment.
  • Shiprocket and its courier partners — receive your name, phone number and delivery address to deliver your parcel.
  • Supabase — hosts our database and product photos.
  • Vercel — hosts the website.
  • Resend — delivers our order emails.

We never sell your information, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Cookies and local storage

We use one essential cookie for your session and shopping bag, and your browser's local storage for small preferences (such as whether you've already seen today's special). Nothing is used for advertising.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal purposes. You can ask us to delete your account and saved addresses at any time; order records we must keep by law are retained.
